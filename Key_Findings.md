# Key Findings — Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis

- **Total Revenue:** ₹14,957,505.27 generated across all outlets analysed, from 7,060 items.

- **Fat Content Preference:** Low Fat items generate more revenue overall, at ₹9,583,492.58 vs ₹5,374,012.69 for Regular items — roughly 64% of total revenue comes from Low Fat products, reflecting both stronger sales per item and a larger share of items stocked.

- **Top Revenue-Generating Category:** Fruits and Vegetables is the best-selling category by revenue, generating ₹2,219,977.61.

- **Best Outlet Size:** Small outlets are the most profitable, generating ₹4,386,518.10 in total sales with an average of ₹2,358.34 per item.

- **Best Performing Location Tier:** Tier 2 cities outperform the others, generating ₹6,472,313.71 in total sales.

- **Best Performing Outlet Type:** Supermarket Type1 outlets lead by a wide margin, generating ₹12,917,342.26 in total revenue from 5,577 items sold, at an average of ₹2,316.18 per item — over 86% of total company revenue comes from this single outlet type.

- **Optimal Price Range:** Items priced in the Premium (₹100–200) range drive the highest total sales, generating ₹8,655,742.22 from 3,844 items.

- **Outlet Age Trend:** No strong correlation between outlet establishment year and sales — outlets from most years (1987–2009) generate similar revenue in the ₹2.0M–2.3M range with comparable item counts (~926-932 items). The one exception is outlets established in 1998, which show significantly lower total sales (₹188,340.17) from far fewer items (555), likely due to differences in outlet type or size rather than age itself.

## Business Recommendation

Blinkit should prioritise expanding and stocking **Supermarket Type1** outlets, since this single format drives the overwhelming majority of total revenue. Within these outlets, focus should go toward **Premium-priced items (₹100–200)** and high-performing categories like **Fruits and Vegetables**, particularly in **Tier 2 cities**, which already show the strongest location-wise sales. The 1998-established outlet cohort's unusually low performance is worth a closer look — it points to a small-format or under-stocked outlet issue rather than a genuine "older outlets underperform" trend.
